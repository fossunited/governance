# Governing Board Election Working Group

## Background

Two successful elections have been carried out for the FOSS United Governing Board -
April 2025 and September 2026. Both times, apart from a little volunteering help
from Ram Iyengar, Staff (Rahul and Ashlesh) have been responsible for all 
decisions related to the election. This includes everything from the timeline
of the election, the questions on the nomination form, scheduling the online
interaction with the candidates, and so on.

With the election process happening before and during IndiaFOSS, we realized
that it would be best to have an independent Election Working Group. Focusing
exclusively on the elections, the WG can ensure that they can follow through
with the election timeline.

## Mission

The Election Working Group ensures fair elections for the FOSS United Governing Board.

## Scope

The Elections WG will

* come up with the schedule for the annual election. They will publish the
  schedule at least 4 months ahead of the date of election for feedback from
  the Staff, Governing Board, and the Community. The schedule will be
  finalized at least 3 months ahead of the date of election.
* define the nomination form, including questions on the form. This will be
  published at the same time as the schedule for feedback
* spread the message about the upcoming election. This includes coordinating
  with Staff members for help with social media outreach, working with volunteers
  to put together visual assets if necessary, and so on.
* coordinate with the IndiaFOSS Programme co-chairs to incorporate elections-related
  sessions into the festival schedule. This includes sessions introducing the
  candidates running for election, physical information about the elections
  during the festival, and so on.
* choose the digital platform that will be used to submit and show nomination forms,
  and the digital platform that will be used for the election.
* put together the voter list, and communicate (e.g. emails) with the eligible voters about the election 
* run the election.

## Membership

The Election WG will consist of three members - one Staff member from the FOSS United
Foundation, one Governing Board member, and one active member of the Indian
FOSS Community. The WG will be constituted at least 6 months ahead of the tentative
election date (IndiaFOSS weekend), and it will be dissolved one month after the
election is completed. No member of the Election WG will run in the upcoming
election.

## Appendix

### History

For the 2025 GB elections, the custom [Ballot](https://github.com/fossunited/ballot) platform was used.
Ballot allowed people to submit a [nomination form](https://fossunited.org/ballot/election/2025-gb-nominations)
and to vote on the election. Ballot was discontinued for elections usage 
after the 2025 GB election due to a significant number of security and UI/UX
issues that were reported, both by the candidates and the candidates running
for eleciton. The 2025 election used the [Scottish STV](https://opavote.com/methods/single-transferable-vote#scottish-stv]
method to count the results. Anyone who had an account on fossunited.org before the
election was considered as an eligible voter.

The following questions were asked in the 2025 nomination form

```
- Give us a brief introduction about yourself
- How many years have you been associated with open source?
- Are you actively involved in existing FOSS United activities? Please specify.
```

For the 2026 GB elections, the [Discourse forum](https://forum.fossunited.org/c/2026-elections/55)
was used to gather nomination forms. A new "2026 GB Elections" category was created
on the forum, interested candidates were asked to create a new topic under this
category using the template nomination format. As for the election itself, after much
deliberation, [bettervoting](https://bettervoting.com/) was used to send the ballots
to eligible voters, to create the ballot, and tabulate the results. We briefly
considered using the [OpaVote](https://opavote.com/) platform, but we ended up
going with bettervoting - because it is FOSS and it was cheaper to use. We used
the [STAR voting method](https://www.equal.vote/star) to count the results. Due
to a significant amount of spam accounts on fossunited.org in 2026, the eligible
voter list was created using RSVP'd users for FOSS United events and anyone who
ever paid to attend a FOSS United event.

The following questions were asked in the 2026 nomination form

```
- About me
  - Name
  - Bio
  - Location
  - Personal site/ LinkedIn
  - Home community: (Digital Commons community you have longstanding association/interest with)
  - Years of association with FOSS/digital commons
- About my work
  - My FOSS/digital commons journey
  - How and when I came across the FOSS United community
  - Why I am running for the Governing Board
  - Why would I be a good fit (What prior long-term volunteering experience do you have)
  - Things I would like to focus on as a GB member
  - Optional: Anything else you’d like to share with the community. For instance, what impact or change do you want to bring about in the FOSS United or the Indian FOSS community?
```

Results from both elections were uploaded to [GitHub](https://github.com/fossunited/elections/).
