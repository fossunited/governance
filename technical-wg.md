# Technical Working Group Charter

Version : 0.1

Status : DRAFT. Open to comments. Not yet adopted.

# Background

FOSS United Foundation runs community events and aids community initiatives and projects.
A variety of technical artifacts are being used by FOSSU for this purpose. Transitioning
the ownership of most of these to the community is an important aspect of the move to a
community-led model of operation.

# Mission

The Technical Working Group (TWG) exists to define, own, develop, and maintain technology,
infrastructure and services deployed by FOSS United Foundation for the community.

# Scope

TWG collectively provides leadership to ensure that FOSSU uses the best development,
deployment, maintenance and sustenance practices in all technical matters.

TWG's scope includes:

- Physical and virtual infrastructure
- Software - including third-party paid subscriptions
- Software based services provided to the community
- Shared hardware (e.g storage media, accessories, and devices) or hardware owned for
  usage in events.
- Managing the budget, spend and decisions for these aspects
- Community accessible documentation related to these aspects

Technical aspects that are purely internal to FOSS United Foundation (i.e. those without
any community and volunteer touchpoint) are out of scope of the TWG. Examples would be
any software that's used internally including communication platforms (e.g. mattermost),
IT hardware used by employees, etc.

# Activities 

Primary activities of the TWG shall be:

* Driving technical artifacts to meet the needs and requirements of the community
* Enlisting and coordinating volunteers to develop and operate artifacts
* Define and execute a transition path of various artifacts from the foundation to the community.
* Quarterly and annual updates to the community
* Preparing budget and managing expenses

Members of the TWG are expected to possess demonstrable ability to "own" projects. Primarily,
this would boil down to taking appropriate medium to long term goals. Strong focus on long
term maintenance and sustainability would be highly desirable in technical decisions (as
opposed to going with the "latest" or the "in thing").  Execution wise, TWG must strive to
continuously improve/keep documentation up-to-date and take any and all measures to aid
and encourage community involvement in technical activities.

# Membership

To start off, a consensus based governance is envisioned for the TWG, a "mixture of interested
experts" kind of model.

The workgroup shall consist of a minimum of five members from the community.  Additionally, all
FOSSU staff who are managing technical artifacts in TWG scope shall be members of the workgroup.

Members of the community may self nominate themselves on the forum. The nomination must include
the areas of interest and expertise. Each nomination must be backed by at-least one member of
the community.  Backing must include a specific reason for backing.  If the reason provided
is not adequate, any member of the GB may ask backer to clarify the reason for backing.

# Method of Operation

TWG will run based on consultations, discussions and consensus.  If consensus is not achieved,
then the group may approach the governance board for guidance.

Independent sub-groups of the TWG may be formed based on common interests and areas of expertis.
Sub-groups will put down roadmaps, activities, initiatives, rationale, etc. in a community
visible location. To begin with, floating Request for Comments on the Forum is highly recommended,
with a 2 week period for comments. This would then be followed by discussions, decisions based
on consensus, and execution of the stated plan.

Before the beginning of a financial year, the TWG is expected to put out a budgetary plan for the
next year addressing requirement for funds, areas of spending, and anticipated activities.

# Standards of Discourse

A high degree of professional conduct is expected. Opinions are expected to be backed by solid
reasons. Personal attacks must strictly be avoided.

# Changes to the Charter

TWG 0.1 is a starting point to provide sufficient enablement to begin, and a direction.

After initial adoption, it is expected that the charter will be amended as specifics of the
work group get nailed down (e.g. need for voting, specific powers such as commit rights).
Amendments to the charter must increment the version number.  As subgroups grow in size,
and become more specialized, we expect the subgroups to create a new working group.
